Load your own 26.x client .jar (macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/versions/26.2/26.2.jar, Windows: %appdata%\.minecraft\versions\26.2\26.2.jar) then add any resource packs on top. Everything stays in this browser. You can also drop files onto the page.

Stack packs like in Minecraft: your .jar at the bottom, resource packs above it. Higher in the list wins.
